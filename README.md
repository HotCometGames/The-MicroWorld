This was one of my first big (as in a decent amount of content and code made for it) games.
I made it in the fall of 2024, so my sophomore year, and I had watched a kurzgesagt in a nutshell video about the microworld, and the wars our body goes through protecting us.
With me also being a big fan of pokemon, and loving the idea of a procedural infinite game, I mixed all of them together.
This was my first time dealing with procedural generation, which is why it uses such a simple form, along with procedural animation.

I hope you enjoy it! (to any MIT reviewers lurking around)
